### Checklist for every Reel

1. Upload the finished 9:16 video, with the on-screen text already added in CapCut or Edits.
2. Cover: pick the strongest frame, or upload the first-frame image with the on-screen text.
3. Caption: copy the "Post caption" line from `content-plan.md`, including its 3 to 5 hashtags.
4. **Turn ON "AI label"** on the last screen. This is required for realistic AI video.
5. Music: none. The voice is the audio.
6. No location tag. Keep real places out.
7. Share to the feed: on.
