Content plan for Rosa, 94: health and healthy aging, the Sicilian widow who swims in the sea every morning.

## The 8 ideas, ranked

**#1: Things I never do at 94 (series)**
- **Hook (first line she says):** "Things I never do, and I'm ninety-four."
- **Format and hold:**
  - Listicle, as a weekly series.
  - Each "never" is funnier than the last, and one item collapses mid-list.
  - The ending opens a loop for the next part.
- **Setting:** tiny whitewashed Sicilian kitchen, early morning, a dented moka pot on the stove, Rosa at the table with the white crochet cloth.
- **Why it works:** "Things I'd never do at [age]" is the signature format of the biggest grandparent creators. At 94, every "never" is a flex, and it repeats forever.

**#2: Swim with Rosa (series)**
- **Hook:** "Ninety-four, and I just came out of the sea."
- **Format and hold:**
  - Day-in-the-life, direct address, straight after the swim.
  - Holds on her answer to "isn't it cold?" and lands on the catchphrase.
- **Setting:** flat pale limestone rocks by turquoise water, the old metal ladder, early light; Rosa with wet hair and a striped towel round her shoulders.
- **Why it works:** this is the whole account in one image: the contrast is visible before she speaks. Morning-routine content is a top performer in wellness, and this one is a ritual she can repeat in every season.

**#3: My doctor is sixty years younger than me**
- **Hook:** "My doctor is sixty years younger than me."
- **Format and hold:**
  - Storytime.
  - Holds on what the doctor says every year, then a double punchline.
- **Setting:** narrow ochre alley, Rosa on a low wooden stool by her blue front door, geraniums beside her.
- **Why it works:** a specific number in the first five words, a role reversal and a gentle joke. It's highly shareable, and it's safe: she listens to her doctor.

**#4: If you're thirty and tired every morning**
- **Hook:** "Thirty and tired every morning? Listen to me."
- **Format and hold:**
  - POV, direct callout.
  - Holds on her "big secret", which turns out to be tiny.
  - Soft plug for the Morning Reset.
- **Setting:** the kitchen, morning light, the phone propped against the bowl of lemons.
- **Why it works:** a direct callout of the audience's most common morning complaint, said by someone three times their age. It leads straight to the product.

**#5: Red flags in your morning**
- **Hook:** "Red flags in your morning, from a woman who's had thirty-four thousand mornings."
- **Format and hold:**
  - Signs / red flags.
  - The list collapses at number three, then a "green flag" twist ending.
- **Setting:** the alley doorstep in the morning, Rosa on the stool with a small cup of coffee.
- **Why it works:** red-flag content is a proven format across niches. Here it's twisted from dating into morning habits, and the oddly exact number is the hook.

**#6: What I eat in a day at 94 (silent b-roll)**
- **Hook (on-screen):** "What I eat in a day at 94"
- **Format and hold:**
  - Silent text-over b-roll; the text is added in post.
  - Loop-friendly.
  - Holds on the Sunday glass of wine line at the end.
- **Setting:** the kitchen, Rosa pouring coffee from the dented moka into a small cup and settling back to look at the sea.
- **Why it works:** "What I eat in a day" is one of the most searched formats in health. At 94 it's pure curiosity, and silent clips are cheap and rewatchable.

**#7: My husband bought me this with one fish**
- **Hook:** "My husband bought me this with one fish."
- **Format and hold:**
  - Interview / storytime, answering Giulia off camera.
  - Holds on the swordfish story, with a quiet last line.
- **Setting:** the alley doorstep in the evening, Rosa in her blue housedress and grey cardigan.
- **Why it works:** grandparent love stories drive saves and shares. It gives the account a heart, and it ties to her coral-bead anchor.

**#8: Riposo: you call it lazy**
- **Hook:** "You call it lazy. My whole town does it at two o'clock."
- **Format and hold:**
  - Contrarian take on hustle culture.
  - Holds on the image of the dogs in the street and the "meeting about another meeting" line.
- **Setting:** the alley doorstep in the afternoon shade.
- **Why it works:** it pushes back on hustle and productivity culture. Rest is a rising wellness theme, and a 94-year-old defending naps is a picture people stop for.

**Shoot first:**
- **#1:** the series opener with the strongest hook.
- **#2:** the visual that explains the whole account.
- **#4:** gets the product's problem into people's heads before the store link exists.

## Hook rules (use for every new Rosa video)

1. **Frame one shows the situation**: the sea, the fog, the scar, the object. Rosa is already mid-action, never a neutral talking head.
2. **The first spoken sentence holds the tension** in about 8 words or fewer: a decision ("I'm not going in"), a contradiction ("The sea is perfect. I'm not going in."), or a surprising fact ("An octopus did this.").
3. **The on-screen text is a statement, never a question someone asks her**: 8 words or fewer, with "94", a number or "never" when it fits, and the same idea as the first spoken line.
4. **The reason lands by second 5.** Then one concrete detail, one line she'd say again, and a one-word close ("Ciao.").
5. **One idea per video.** No set-up line ("Giulia says...", "Nonna, tell them...") before the hook.
6. **The book is a character, not an ad.** At most two videos in five mention it, in Rosa's own words (a day number, a rule, her handwritten notebook, "it's in the bio"), never a price and never a URL. The product caption line goes only under those videos, and only once the store link is in the bio.

## Rules every script was checked against

- **Spoken length:** 40 to 110 spoken words, mostly 72 to 81, so each fits a 30-second generation.
- **Human-sounding:**
  - No balanced rule-of-three lists, and no thesis lines.
  - One self-interruption or trail-off per script.
  - One uselessly specific detail per script (the goat beard, the seagull, the ugly car).
  - One near-repetition per script.
  - Uneven rhythm.
- **Safety:**
  - No health claims, cures or diet prescriptions.
  - Swimming is always "where it's safe, never alone".
  - Doctors are respected.
- **Before the store exists:** batch-1 CTAs never point to a link that doesn't exist yet. Cut the link line in R4 for day 4.
