**What's inside:**

- A 48-page printable A4 PDF, with amount columns that work in any currency
- **The Leak Hunt:** an eight-step guided sitting, with fill-in pages for what comes in, regular bills, subscriptions, small daily spends and what's owed. Every outgoing gets the question "Would I buy this again today?"
- **Ray's Rules:** seven named rules, each with the story behind it, an honest note on where it doesn't fit, and a small job to try that week
- **Sunday Sums:** the fifteen-minute weekly ritual step by step, Ray's own filled-in example, and twelve weeks of identical fill-in pages
- **A Money Calm Score** to take before you start and again after Week 12, so you can see what changed
- **Honest adaptations** for couples, irregular income, very little coming in, and debt. Both payoff methods are explained and you pick. It also lists where to find free, impartial debt help
- **Five extras:** the Twenty-Minute Leak Hunt, a cut-out fridge card, the Shed Door List (the Thirty-Day Wait), Things I've Never Paid For, and Ray's annotated notebook page from March 1987
- **Not included:** financial advice, product recommendations or promised savings
